确权对象：唯事件

统一以「客观发生的历史事件」为确权核心，而非传统的资产/代币，回归事实本身的价值。

事实记录无资金门槛，任何人只需支付Gas费即可铸造Event NFT，人人都能成为事实的见证者。

价值机制：引用税

事件价值不依赖炒作，而是由被相信、被使用的次数决定，引用越多，价值越高。

生态定位：重型事实层

专注高价值重型事实锚定，需多源证据、物理验证，成为Web3世界的「事实基础设施」。

Not Proof of Asset, But Proof of Event
What Happens, Exists; What's Proven, Trusted

STProof (Spatial-Temporal Proof) is a universal on-chain event proof and value capture protocol, redefining the underlying logic of on-chain fact certification with equity at its core.

Whitepaper v5.2

Core Concepts

Equity-centered, redefining on-chain proof, making facts the trusted infrastructure of the digital world

Proof Object: Event Only

Focus on objectively occurring historical events as the core of proof, rather than traditional assets/tokens, returning to the intrinsic value of facts themselves.

Core Philosophy: Equitable Proof

Zero financial barriers for fact recording. Anyone can mint Event NFTs by paying only gas fees, making everyone a witness of facts.

Value Mechanism: Citation Royalty

Event value does not rely on speculation, but on the number of times it is trusted and used — the more citations, the higher the value.

Ecosystem Position: Heavy Fact Layer

Focus on anchoring high-value heavy facts requiring multi-source evidence and physical verification, becoming the "fact infrastructure" of the Web3 world.

Event Types

Tangible Events

Typical Scenarios

First day cover mailing, art creation, cultural relic excavation, porcelain firing, physical commodity production and other events requiring physical verification.

Intangible Events

Typical Scenarios

Online releases, on-chain voting, opinion statements, academic publications, code submissions and other purely digital events.

Hybrid Events

Typical Scenarios

News scenes, performances, legal evidence preservation, offline meetings and other events combining physical and digital forms.
